## November 9, 2026 - Minor Release

### Drop IP-option SYNs

The node [Advanced Options]({{<relref "docs/nodes/appliances/advanced#config-options" >}}) panel now includes **Drop IP-option SYNs** for nodes running the [November 2026 Minor Appliance release]({{<relref "release-notes/node/2026-11" >}}) or newer. It is part of the [CVE-2026-98432 mitigations]({{<relref "release-notes/node/2026-11#cve-2026-98432-mitigations" >}}) in that release.
